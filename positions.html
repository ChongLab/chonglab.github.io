Postdoctoral position in Bioinformatics 

Postdoctoral positions are available in Dr. Zechen Chong’s Laboratory in the Department of Genome Sciences, School of Medicine, University of Virginia (UVA). We are seeking highly motivated and creative researchers with a passion for computational genomics and bioinformatics to join our growing team.

Our research focuses on developing innovative computational methods for analyzing large-scale genomic data and applying these approaches to address fundamental questions in human genetics and disease. Current research areas include algorithm development for long-read sequencing, structural variation discovery, pangenomics, and the integration of emerging sequencing technologies such as PacBio HiFi, Oxford Nanopore, single-cell, and other multi-omics platforms.

Postdoctoral researchers will have the opportunity to lead independent projects while collaborating with investigators at UVA and across national and international genomics initiatives. Projects will be tailored to the interests and expertise of the successful candidate, providing a strong foundation for career development in computational genomics. Initial appointments are for 2–3 years, with the possibility of extension based on performance and funding.

Ideal candidates

  • Hold a Ph.D. in genomics, bioinformatics, computer science, physics, mathematics, genetics, or a related field, with a background in computational biology.
  • Have strong skills in algorithm design and data analysis; experience with next-generation or long-read sequencing is a plus.
  • Demonstrate relevant research achievements.

How to apply

Please email Zechen Chong a letter of interest and current CV, including a publication list and contact details for three references.


About the University of Virginia

Founded in 1819 by Thomas Jefferson, the University of Virginia (UVA) is one of the nation’s top public research universities. The UVA School of Medicine is internationally recognized for excellence in biomedical research, clinical care, and medical education, and consistently ranks among the top public medical schools in the United States for research and NIH funding.

The Department of Genome Sciences fosters a collaborative and interdisciplinary research environment spanning genomics, genetics, computational biology, biomedical informatics, and precision medicine. Researchers benefit from close collaborations across the Schools of Medicine, Engineering and Applied Science, Data Science, and Arts & Sciences, as well as access to state-of-the-art core facilities, high-performance computing resources, and advanced genomics technologies.

Located in Charlottesville, Virginia, UVA combines a world-class research environment with an exceptional quality of life. Charlottesville is a vibrant college town in the foothills of the Blue Ridge Mountains, offering a rich cultural scene, abundant outdoor recreation, and an affordable cost of living. The city is conveniently located within a two-hour drive of Washington, D.C., providing easy access to one of the nation’s largest hubs for biomedical research, biotechnology, and federal research agencies, including the NIH.

Together, these strengths make UVA an outstanding place to pursue cutting-edge research, develop scientific independence, and build a successful academic or industry career.


Graduate students

We are seeking highly motivated Ph.D. students who are passionate about computational genomics, bioinformatics, and data science. Students in the lab receive interdisciplinary training in algorithm development, software engineering, statistical genomics, and large-scale genomic data analysis, while working closely with collaborators across the University of Virginia and the broader genomics community.

Projects

Graduate students will develop and apply computational methods to analyze diverse genomic datasets generated by next-generation and long-read sequencing technologies, including PacBio HiFi, Oxford Nanopore, single-cell, pangenome, and other emerging multi-omics platforms. Research projects are tailored to each student’s interests and provide opportunities to contribute to both methodological advances and biological discoveries.

Required skills

  • Strong motivation for research
  • A strong background in algorithm design and analysis
  • Strong programming skills
  • Clear written and spoken communication skills

How to apply

Please send a letter of intent and your CV to Zechen Chong.
